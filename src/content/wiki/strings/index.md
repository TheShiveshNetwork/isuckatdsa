### Overview

Hey, guess what. Strings are just arrays that learned to spell. That apartment building from [[arrays]]? Now each door has a letter instead of a number. Everything you just learned still works. The same walks, the same notebook, the same camera. So 90 percent of what you know from arrays you can literally copy paste here.

The only new twist is that letters care about order and identity in a way numbers do not always. Is it the same forwards and backwards? Are these two words just shuffled versions of each other? That is where strings get their own flavour.

Here is your map for this floor, and you will see how many of these are just array tricks in a costume:

- [[strings/palindrome-check]] - reading the same forwards and backwards, two walkers meeting in the middle
- [[strings/anagram-check]] - same letters, different order, thank you [[hashing/frequency-map]]
- [[strings/sliding-window-on-strings]] - your camera frame from [[arrays/sliding-window]] but now the train is made of letters
- [[strings/group-anagrams]] - sorting words into families that share the same letters
- [[strings/advanced-string-matching]] - KMP and rolling hash when you need to find a needle fast

If you felt good on the train, you will feel good here. Same building, just fancier doors.
